# Northstar Digital Modernization Portfolio — Fictional Charter
Executive sponsor: Maya Chen, COO. Portfolio owner: Daniel Brooks, VP, Enterprise Platforms. Monthly reporting; reporting month is September 2026.

Objectives:
1. Improve customer and employee digital service reliability.
2. Reduce manual effort and service turnaround.
3. Improve data quality and management visibility.
4. Deliver measurable benefits within approved funding.

Projects:
- P-101 Customer Portal Renewal — Elena Ruiz — approved budget $1,200k.
- P-102 Data Platform Modernization — Marcus Lee — approved budget $900k.
- P-103 Service Workflow Automation — Priya Nair — approved budget $650k.
- P-104 Identity & Access Upgrade — Jordan Blake — approved budget $500k.

Rules:
- Currency is USD thousands unless stated otherwise.
- Actual spend is booked spend; forecast at completion (FAC) is a forecast.
- Forecast variance = FAC − approved budget; positive means over budget.
- Forecast variance % = variance / approved budget × 100.
- Cumulative actual consumed % = cumulative actual / approved budget × 100.
- Monthly burn % = month's spend / approved budget × 100. It is not a completion percentage.
- Benefits are forecast unless explicitly marked realized/validated.
- Do not invent risk scores or infer approvals.
