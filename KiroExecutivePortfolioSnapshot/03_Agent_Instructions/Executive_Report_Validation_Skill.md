# Executive Report Validation
1. Confirm exactly four sections in the required order.
2. Count exactly three sentences in Executive Summary.
3. Recalculate variance, variance %, cumulative actual consumed %, and monthly burn % from the latest finance snapshot.
4. Compare like-for-like July/August/September measures; do not confuse monthly and cumulative spend.
5. Verify every executive blocker against risk/resource/decision registers.
6. Verify decision owner, deadline, status, and consequence against the decision register.
7. Do not turn forecast benefits into realized benefits.
8. Flag unsupported claims, missing evidence, stale sources, and unresolved conflicts.
9. Correct or disclose issues before returning the report.
