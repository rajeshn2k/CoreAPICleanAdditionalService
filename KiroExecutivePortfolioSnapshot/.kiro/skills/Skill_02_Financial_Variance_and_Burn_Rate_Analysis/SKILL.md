# Skill 2 — Financial Variance and Burn-Rate Analysis

## Purpose
Calculate financial variance and budget consumption only from comparable, traceable financial inputs.

## Procedure
1. Distinguish approved budget, actual expenditure, forecast, estimate, and business-case target.
2. Confirm scope, currency, reporting cutoff, and period before comparing values.
3. Show formulas and source inputs for material calculations.
4. Flag missing values, scope changes, mismatched periods, currency differences, and conflicting sources.
5. Do not equate spend with progress or value delivered; do not calculate ROI without an approved definition and required inputs.

## Calculation rules
- Cost variance for the same scope and period = Actual cost − approved budget for that scope and period.
- Forecast overrun = Forecast final cost − approved total budget.
- Forecast variance % = (Forecast final cost − approved total budget) ÷ approved total budget × 100.
- Budget consumed % = Actual expenditure ÷ approved budget for the matching scope and period × 100.
- Average monthly burn rate = expenditure over the stated number of months ÷ number of months. State the period.
- If the denominator is zero, missing, or incomparable, report the calculation as unavailable.

## Required output
Project, budget and period, actuals and data date, forecast final cost, variance amount/percentage, budget consumed %, burn rate and period, currency/scope, sources, and data-quality warnings.

## Guardrails
Never fill missing values with zero. Do not mix currencies without an approved conversion basis. Do not compare year-to-date actuals with a full-year budget as if periods match. Do not update financial systems.

## Completion check
Every calculation has traceable inputs, formula, currency, scope, and period.
