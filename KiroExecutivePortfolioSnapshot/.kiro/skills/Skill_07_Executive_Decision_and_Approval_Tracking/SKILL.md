# Skill 7 — Executive Decision and Approval Tracking

## Purpose
Extract pending portfolio decisions requiring sponsor, steering committee, or executive approval.

## Procedure
1. Review authorized decision logs, steering minutes, funding/change requests, and governance records.
2. Distinguish Proposed, Pending, Approved, Rejected, Deferred, and Unknown.
3. Capture the exact decision/sign-off requested and approving authority where evidenced.
4. Include options and consequences only when documented.
5. Record deadline, affected projects, financial/strategic implications, and source.
6. Check for newer authoritative records that supersede older decisions.
7. Flag missing or conflicting status, approver, or deadline.
8. Use a Yes/No question only when that accurately represents the documented decision.

## Required output
Decision request, affected projects, status, Yes/No or sign-off request if supported, documented options/consequences, implications, decision maker/forum, deadline, source/date, and gaps.

## Guardrails
Never mark a pending decision approved. Do not invent approvers, deadlines, options, or consequences. Do not approve/reject requests or commit funding, scope, dates, or resources.

## Completion check
Each decision has a clear evidence-based request and status; missing authority/timing is visible.
