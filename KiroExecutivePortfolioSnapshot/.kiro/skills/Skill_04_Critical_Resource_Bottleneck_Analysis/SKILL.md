# Skill 4 — Critical Resource Bottleneck Analysis

## Purpose
Identify evidenced capacity shortages, scarce skills, competing demand, and cross-project allocation conflicts that materially affect outcomes.

## Procedure
1. Identify the role/skill, capacity, demand, time period, and affected projects.
2. Compare supply and demand only when units and periods match.
3. Connect the constraint to a milestone, cost, deliverable, or objective only where evidence supports the link.
4. Record existing mitigation and whether it is approved, proposed, or unknown.
5. Determine whether resolution needs project-level action, portfolio coordination, or executive intervention using documented governance rules.
6. Treat unconfirmed constraints as provisional.

## Required output
Constraint, affected projects, capacity/demand and period if available, related milestone, documented consequence, mitigation/status, intervention required, owner/deadline if evidenced, source, and confidence.

## Guardrails
Do not invent capacity figures, allocation percentages, names, shortages, or impacts. Do not assess employee performance from messages, attendance, response times, or task counts. Do not reassign resources or commit funding.

## Completion check
Each bottleneck is evidence-based and distinguishes documented impact from potential impact.
