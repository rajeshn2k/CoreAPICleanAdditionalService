# Skill 5 — Enterprise Risk and Escalation Prioritization

## Purpose
Identify risks/issues that cross project boundaries, threaten strategic outcomes, or require action beyond delegated project authority.

## Procedure
1. Review authorized risk, issue, blocker, and governance records.
2. Identify cross-project effects, enterprise exposure, strategic consequences, material financial exposure, and decisions requiring executive authority.
3. Apply the organization's approved risk framework and escalation thresholds when available.
4. If no approved framework exists, do not invent numerical scores or thresholds; describe the evidence and mark escalation level for review.
5. Distinguish risks (possible future events) from issues (existing conditions).
6. Record mitigation, residual exposure, owner, due date, and status where evidenced.
7. Exclude routine operational items unless material portfolio impact or authority limits are documented.

## Required output
Risk/issue, affected projects/objectives, source/date, approved rating/framework if available, documented impact, mitigation/status, requested intervention, owner/authority/deadline if evidenced, confidence, and gaps.

## Guardrails
Do not invent probability, impact, scores, or thresholds. Do not present potential impact as certain or change official ratings. Do not make executive decisions.

## Completion check
Each escalation has a source-backed reason for executive attention.
