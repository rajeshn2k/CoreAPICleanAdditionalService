# Skill 1 — Portfolio Evidence Consolidation

## Purpose
Collect and reconcile project-level evidence from approved sources for a complete, traceable portfolio view.

## Procedure
1. Identify the authoritative portfolio/project register and reporting cutoff.
2. Gather evidence only from user-provided or explicitly authorized sources.
3. Capture project, finding, source, source date, reporting period, and confidence for each material fact.
4. Detect missing projects, duplicate records, stale data, inconsistent definitions, incomparable reporting periods, and conflicting values.
5. Prefer an organization-designated authoritative source. If a conflict remains unresolved, report it instead of choosing silently.
6. Never treat missing data as zero or as proof that no risk/change exists.
7. Classify findings as Confirmed, Provisional, Conflicting, or Unknown.

## Required output
Evidence register with: project, finding/metric, classification, reporting period/data date, source, conflict/freshness note, missing information, confidence rationale, and executive relevance.

## Guardrails
Do not invent projects, figures, owners, dates, or statuses. Do not modify source records or expose restricted information.

## Completion check
Every material finding is traceable; portfolio coverage gaps and source conflicts are visible.
