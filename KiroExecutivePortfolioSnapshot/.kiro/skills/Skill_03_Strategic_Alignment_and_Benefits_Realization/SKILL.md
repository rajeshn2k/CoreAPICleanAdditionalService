# Skill 3 — Strategic Alignment and Benefits Realization

## Purpose
Compare project outcomes with approved strategic objectives and business-case benefits, distinguishing expected benefits from realized results.

## Procedure
1. Identify each project's approved strategic objective.
2. Extract documented benefit targets, baselines, measures, target dates, and owners.
3. Find realized results and measurement dates.
4. Separate approved targets, forecasts, estimates, and realized outcomes.
5. Compare target and actual only when definitions, units, scope, and periods are compatible.
6. Mark benefits as Target, Forecast, Realized, Not Measured, Conflicting, or Unknown.
7. Calculate ROI only when an approved method and consistent cost/benefit inputs are available.

## Required output
Project, strategic objective, expected benefit/target, measure and target date, realized result and date, variance if calculable, benefit status, source, confidence, and data gaps.

## Guardrails
Do not infer benefit realization from feature delivery. Do not present forecasts as realized results. Do not invent ROI, attribution, baselines, targets, or strategic alignment.

## Completion check
Targets and realized outcomes are clearly separated and material claims are source-backed.
