# Follow-up prompts — run individually

1. **Budget ranking:** Rank projects by September forecast variance %, highest overrun first. Show budget, FAC, variance $, variance %, cumulative actual, and source.
2. **Three-month trend:** Compare July/August/September forecast variance per project. Which deteriorated or improved, and what source-backed explanations exist?
3. **Resource conflict:** Explain the P-101/P-102 conflict, documented capacity gaps, impacts, and exact pending decisions; cite resource/risk/decision files.
4. **Upcoming decisions:** List decisions due on or before 15 October 2026 with ID, request, owner, status, deadline, and consequence.
5. **Strategic benefits:** Which benefits are expected and which realized? Separate forecast from validated actual and identify evidence gaps.
6. **Delivery status:** Summarize status changes July–September, separating recorded status from interpretation.
7. **Executive-only escalation:** Include only blockers requiring executive intervention or budget approval; exclude project-level issues.
8. **Evidence audit:** For each material claim in the September report, list supporting file and record/field; flag unsupported claims.
9. **Change since August:** Summarize material September changes in financial forecast, milestones, resource constraints, and pending decisions. Do not invent August decision status.
10. **Decision scenario:** Based only on the decision log, explain the documented consequence of delaying DEC-01. Do not invent a revised numerical forecast.
